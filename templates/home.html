Loan Approval Criteria

Our system evaluates your application based on these key factors:

✅ Approved
  • Credit score ≥ 720
  • Debt-to-Income ratio < 30%
  • Loan amount < 2× annual income
🔄 Under Review
  • Credit score 600–719
  • Debt-to-Income ratio 30–50%
  • Loan officer review required
❌ Rejected
  • Credit score < 600
  • Debt-to-Income ratio > 50%
  • High risk financial profile
ℹ️ What is DTI Ratio?

DTI = (Existing Debt ÷ Annual Income) × 100. A lower DTI means you have less financial burden and are more likely to repay.
